Privacy note

Useful tracking, without unnecessary exposure.

Restock uses the minimum account, pantry, renewal, and approval information needed to predict what needs attention and complete actions you explicitly approve.

What Restock stores

Restock may store your account identifier, tracked items, purchase or renewal dates, cadence estimates, spending limits, notification choices, workflow state, and a redacted audit history. If you sign in with Google, Restock verifies the credential returned by Google; it never receives your Google password.

Payment boundaries

Raw card numbers, CVV values, and one-time payment credentials are not stored in your Restock profile or audit log. Payment authorization is handled through Prava and the relevant merchant surface. Restock records only the references and outcomes needed to resume and explain a workflow.

How information is used

  • Predict depletion and upcoming renewals.
  • Show current decisions, spending boundaries, and audit history.
  • Send approved notifications and prevent duplicate actions.
  • Reconcile an explicitly approved checkout or renewal.

Service providers

Restock shares only the information required for a requested step with its infrastructure, authentication, notification, payment, and merchant providers. Each affected workflow displays whether a capability is real, sandboxed, or simulated.

Your controls

You can skip a proposed action, remove tracked items, sign out, and request an export or deletion of your Restock data. Account deletion and export requests are handled through the project’s published support channel while the product is in controlled access.
