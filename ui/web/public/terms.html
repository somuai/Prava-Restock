Terms of use

Restock proposes. You remain in control.

Restock helps monitor essentials and renewals, prepare a proposal, and carry out an action only through the approval and payment boundaries shown in the product.

Controlled access

Restock is currently a controlled-access product. Access may be limited to invited accounts while provider integrations, reliability, and support processes are validated.

Your approvals

You are responsible for reviewing the item, merchant, amount, plan, and delivery details before approving. Adjust, Skip, Renew, and Switch are distinct actions. A generic approval never silently selects a different product or subscription plan.

Operating modes

Some capabilities may operate in real, sandbox, or disclosed-simulation mode. Restock identifies the active mode in the interface and audit history. A sandbox or simulated result is not a real merchant charge or completed purchase.

Availability and prices

Merchant availability, final price, delivery timing, and vendor plan terms can change. Restock revalidates supported quotes before checkout and requires another approval when configured safety limits are exceeded. It does not guarantee stock, delivery, or renewal.

Account security

Keep access to your Google account, recovery credentials, passkeys, and devices secure. Google linking requires proof from both an existing Restock session and a Google sign-in; accounts are never linked from an email match alone.

Acceptable use

Do not use Restock to bypass provider controls, test payment instruments outside their documented environments, automate prohibited purchases, or interfere with another person’s account.
